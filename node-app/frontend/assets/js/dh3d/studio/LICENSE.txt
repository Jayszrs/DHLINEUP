The Blender Studio source pages identify these rigs as CC-BY and require the
credits above. This application adapts their mesh topology, hairstyle and eye
geometry. Modifications: simplified 17-bone deform skeleton, web-ready glTF
export, texture packing/resizing, turquoise/navy clothing materials, poses and
independent workplace animation. These models are illustrative avatars and do
not represent real photographs or assert endorsement by Blender Foundation.

Original source archives are not included in the web application.
